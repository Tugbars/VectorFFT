1The tiling

Different lengths need different machinery, and so do different cache levels. One plan is one point of the product

E = L × C × M × F × T,
(1)

where L is the cache level (4 of them), C the length class (5), M the machinery (8), F the threaded form (5) and T the thread count (4). Of the 160 combinations of L × C × M, 52 exist on this CPU: the boxes of Figure 1. Each tile is one length class at one cache level, each box one machinery the code admits there, and its italic line the threaded forms that can serve it.

small engines: mono, pair chain3 flat DIT ZTURN-T four-step (Bailey) Rader, Bluestein crossroad: two machineries meet

3The other sheets

Each transform kind has a sheet of its own, with its own machinery, and most of them nest cells of this one.

  • R2C and C2R. zr2c (a complex child of N/2 and the fold), ZTT-r (ZTURN-T at N/2 with the fold fused), the real four-step from 220, and for odd N the real flat DIT and the real Bluestein. Threads run the fold and the child's own raced form.
  • 2D and 3D. Rows and columns are cells of this sheet: see the 2D sheet and the 3D sheet.
  • Batches. Contiguous batches run a loop, a queue or groups. Lane-major batches race the column walk, strips or tile, run the Rader stage on live lanes and the turned prime pass, and group a real batch's rows by L1d.
  • DCT and DST. A real FFT is the child: Makhoul's method for DST-II and DST-III, one R2C of twice the length for DCT-I and DST-I.
  • Each CPU, ISA and precision. The CPU moves the columns: the 9800X3D has a 1 MiB L2 and a 96 MiB L3. The ISA moves the edges: FP64 ZTURN-T starts at 16 points on AVX2 and at 64 on AVX-512. FP32 halves the bytes: ZTURN-T runs to 220, where the four-step starts.

Note. A prime's inner, a real transform's child, the four-step's two sides and a plane's rows and columns are all 1D complex cells. A change in one tile of Figure 1 reaches every tile that nests it.

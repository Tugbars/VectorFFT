1The tiling

An N1 × N2 plane runs a column pass over N1 and a row pass over N2. One plan is one point of the product

E = L × C × M × R × F × T,
(1)

where L is the cache level of the plane (4), C the class of the column length N1 (4), M the column machinery (7), R the row route (4), F the threaded form (8) and T the thread count. Figure 1 draws L × C × M: a tile is one class of N1 at one cache level, a box one way the code may run the columns there, its italic line the threaded forms. The row route follows N2 alone and stands in Table 1.

column chain banded walk skewed pass turn Rader stage, Bluestein, turned prime pass row route crossroad

Note. Bluestein columns won 0 of 396 races on this machine: 2.6× slower or more on small-factor columns, 1.36× or more on prime columns (61, 1,031, 4,099, 4,483). So Bluestein races only a chain that carries a Rader stage.

The row pass is raced with the column verdict, on the whole transform. Threaded rows run over row slabs, one clone of the row plan a worker.

3The other sheets

Each 2D kind has a sheet of its own, and most of them reuse the boxes of Figure 1.

  • 2D real (R2C, C2R). The rows are raced in the row role: the rows kernel (even N2 ≤ 32, the whole pass in one call), real mono, real pair, zr2c, ZTT-r, and for odd N2 the real flat DIT and the real Bluestein. The columns run the machinery of Figure 1 on N2/2 + 1 lanes. C2R is its own plan, with its own verdicts.
  • Lane-major batches. K planes side by side: the columns over every lane (walk, strips or tile, the Rader stage on live lanes, the turned prime pass); a real batch's rows in groups sized from L1d, one block a group where policy names none.
  • Contiguous batches. The plane queue runs one plan K times, as a loop, a queue or groups; the threads take whole planes.
  • The 1D four-step. A 1D length above 262,144 runs as a plane on this machinery: the banded walk with its rows fused, its split raced at T.
  • Each CPU, ISA and precision. L1d sets the one-thread bound: 24 KiB here, 16 KiB on a Zen 4 with 32 KiB. L2 fences the strip widths (N1 × width × 16 B). AVX-512 widens the whole-row kernel to N2 ≤ 64.

Note. The rows are cells of the 1D sheet, and so is the turn's N1 plan. The Rader stage's inner is a chain of p − 1 (1,030 = 2·5·103 nests a second Rader stage), and Bluestein's inner is a 1D cell. A change on the 1D sheet reaches these tiles.

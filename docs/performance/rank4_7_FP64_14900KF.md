# VectorFFT rank 4 to 7 complex transforms — FP64, **Core i9-14900KF**

Complex transforms of rank 4 to 7 on an Intel Core i9-14900KF, AVX2 build, estimate plans, against Intel oneMKL at
1, 4 and 8 threads and against FFTW 3.3.10 at one thread. Speedup = the competitor's time / VectorFFT's time: above
1 means VectorFFT is faster.

- [Against MKL](#against-mkl): 88 shapes, geometric mean **3.09×** at one thread (ahead on 88 of 88), **2.55×** at
  4 threads (87 of 88), **2.46×** at 8 threads (84 of 88).
- [Against FFTW at one thread](#against-fftw-at-one-thread): geometric mean **1.10×**, ahead on 50 of 88. Rank 4 is
  ahead (1.24×), ranks 5 and 6 are level (1.08×, 0.99×), rank 7 is behind (0.93×).
- [Threads](#threads): VectorFFT at 8 threads runs an array of 65,537 to 1 million points 4.9× to 6.5× faster than
  at one thread.

The numbers are **not comparable** with other machines' pages.

## The run

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | Intel Core i9-14900KF, the threads on its 8 P-cores |
| Caches | L1d 48 KiB and L2 2 MiB per P-core, L3 36 MiB shared |
| Library | VectorFFT, branch `fftnd-rank4-7` at commit 68c79e33, AVX2 build for this CPU (`-march=native`), GCC 16.2 |
| Plans | the estimate (`VFFT_FLAG_ESTIMATE`): computed from formulas, nothing measured on this machine, no wisdom read |
| Competitors | Intel oneMKL 2025.3 at the same thread count; FFTW 3.3.10, plans made with `FFTW_MEASURE`, one thread |
| Contract | complex-to-complex FP64, interleaved, natural order, out of place, K = 1 |
| Cells | the four files of [`tests/rank_n/`](../../tests/rank_n/README.md): 36 shapes of rank 4, 21 of rank 5, 17 of rank 6 and 14 of rank 7, from 128 points (2×2×2×2×2×2×2) to 16.7 million (64×64×64×64) |
| Method | the [gauntlet](../../gauntlet/): each cell timed twice with the engine order flipped, the speedup taken from the **worse** of the two; a control cell (16×16×16×16, 8×8×8×8×8, 8×8×8×8×8×8, 4×4×4×4×4×4×4) re-timed through each run |
| Date | 2026-11-22, about 20 minutes |
| Records | [`rank4_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank4_avx2_2026-11-22/), [`rank5_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank5_avx2_2026-11-22/), [`rank6_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank6_avx2_2026-11-22/), [`rank7_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank7_avx2_2026-11-22/): `gauntlet_<rank>d_est.csv` at one thread, `_mt4` and `_mt8` at 4 and 8 threads, `_fftw` against FFTW |

The classes are the cell files': every extent a power of two; extents 2^a × odd; odd extents; a prime above 47 on
one axis. 47 is the largest prime VectorFFT has its own kernel for.

VectorFFT's output and the competitor's differ by at most 4.5e-15 on every cell, relative to the largest value of
the competitor's output.

Where VectorFFT is behind:

- **Extents of 12, 15, 20 and 25.** 20×20×20×20 runs at 0.60×, 15×15×15×15 at 0.80×, 25×25×25×25 at 0.85×. A
  rank-4 transform runs its 3D blocks through the 3D plan, and the cubes of these extents are behind FFTW at rank 3
  with estimate plans too: 20×20×20 at 0.56×, 15×15×15 at 0.80×, 12×12×12 at 0.90×, 25×25×25 at 0.96× (same day,
  same build; the record is
  [`rank3_cubes_fftw_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank3_cubes_fftw_avx2_2026-11-22/)).
- **Many small extents.** The more axes of 2 to 6 points a shape has, the further behind it is:
  2×2×2×2×2×2×2 at 0.48×, 3×3×3×3×3×3×3 and 3×3×3×3×3×3 at 0.73×.

- **A short first axis scales less.** 2×64×64×64 runs 2.2× faster at 8 threads than at one; 64×64×64×4, the same

  L3: 2.7× and 2.3× at 8 threads.

## Running it again

```
python tests/rank_n/make_cells.py
python gauntlet/gauntlet.py run --cells @tests/rank_n/4d-cells.txt --name <run> --bin-dir build/gauntlet --est
python gauntlet/gauntlet.py run --cells @tests/rank_n/4d-cells.txt --name <run> --bin-dir build/gauntlet --est --threads 8
python gauntlet/gauntlet.py run --cells @tests/rank_n/4d-cells.txt --name <run> --bin-dir build/gauntlet --est --cmp fftw
```

One run a rank and thread count; the same `--name` puts a rank's runs in one folder.

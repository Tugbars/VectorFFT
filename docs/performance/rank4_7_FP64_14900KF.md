# VectorFFT rank 4 to 7 transforms — FP64, **Core i9-14900KF**

Complex and real transforms of rank 4 to 7 on an Intel Core i9-14900KF, AVX2 build, estimate plans: one transform
and batches of 4 and 8 transforms against Intel oneMKL at 1, 4 and 8 threads, and complex transforms against FFTW
3.3.10 at one thread. Speedup = the competitor's time / VectorFFT's time: above 1 means VectorFFT is faster.

- [Complex against MKL](#complex-against-mkl): one transform over 88 shapes, geometric mean **3.09×** at one thread
  (ahead on 88 of 88), **2.54×** at 4 threads (87 of 88), **2.48×** at 8 threads (87 of 88). Batches: ahead on
  every lane-major cell; contiguous, ahead on 170 of 170 cells at one thread and 165 of 170 at 8 threads.
- [Real against MKL](#real-against-mkl): one transform, R2C **6.12×**, **4.20×** and **3.80×** at 1, 4 and 8 threads
  (ahead on 88, 88 and 87 of 88), C2R **4.78×**, **3.82×** and **3.84×** (88, 86 and 87 of 88). Batches: ahead on
  every cell at one thread, both layouts; at 8 threads R2C on 170 of 170 contiguous and 114 of 114 lane-major cells, C2R on
  167 of 170 and 110 of 114.
- [Complex against FFTW at one thread](#complex-against-fftw-at-one-thread): geometric mean **1.10×**, ahead on 50 of
  88. Rank 4 is ahead (1.24×), ranks 5 and 6 are level (1.08×, 0.99×), rank 7 is behind (0.93×).
- [Threads](#threads): VectorFFT at 8 threads runs a complex array of 65,537 to 1 million points 4.9× to 6.6×
  faster than at one thread.

The numbers are **not comparable** with other machines' pages.

## The run

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | Intel Core i9-14900KF, the threads on its 8 P-cores |
| Caches | L1d 48 KiB and L2 2 MiB per P-core, L3 36 MiB shared |
| Library | VectorFFT, branch `fftnd-rank4-7` at commit 048569b5, AVX2 build for this CPU (`-march=native`), GCC 16.2 |
| Plans | the estimate (`VFFT_FLAG_ESTIMATE`): computed from formulas, nothing measured on this machine, no wisdom read |
| Competitors | Intel oneMKL 2025.3 at the same thread count; FFTW 3.3.10, plans made with `FFTW_MEASURE`, one thread |
| Contract | FP64, interleaved, natural order, out of place: complex-to-complex, real-to-complex (R2C) and complex-to-real (C2R) |
| Batches | K = 4 and K = 8 transforms a plan, contiguous (one transform after another) and lane-major (the transforms side by side, element by element), against MKL's batch on the same layout; at 1 and 8 threads |
| Cells | the four files of [`tests/rank_n/`](../../tests/rank_n/README.md): 36 shapes of rank 4, 21 of rank 5, 17 of rank 6 and 14 of rank 7, from 128 points (2×2×2×2×2×2×2) to 16.7 million (64×64×64×64). A batch whose K transforms hold more than 2^24 points is not timed. A lane-major real batch needs an even last extent, so it is timed on the shapes that have one |
| Method | the [gauntlet](../../gauntlet/): each cell timed twice with the engine order flipped, the speedup taken from the **worse** of the two; a control cell timed between the others. One thread: pinned to a P-core, its sibling held idle |
| Date | 2026-11-22 |
| Records | [`rank4_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank4_avx2_2026-11-22/), [`rank5_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank5_avx2_2026-11-22/), [`rank6_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank6_avx2_2026-11-22/), [`rank7_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank7_avx2_2026-11-22/). File names: `_r2c` and `_c2r` the real transforms, `_k4` and `_k8` the batches, `_lm` lane-major, `_mt4` and `_mt8` the threads, `_fftw` against FFTW |

The classes are the cell files': every extent a power of two; extents 2^a × odd; odd extents; a prime above 47 on
one axis. 47 is the largest prime VectorFFT has its own kernel for.

VectorFFT's output and the competitor's differ by at most 4.5e-15 on every cell, relative to the largest value of
the competitor's output.

A threaded cell's two readings can differ by a third on this machine. Where one engine order is ahead and the
other behind, both readings are given.

- **Extents of 12, 15, 20 and 25.** 20×20×20×20 runs at 0.60×, 15×15×15×15 at 0.80×, 25×25×25×25 at 0.85×. A
  rank-4 transform runs its 3D blocks through the 3D plan, and the cubes of these extents are behind FFTW at rank 3
  with estimate plans too: 20×20×20 at 0.56×, 15×15×15 at 0.80×, 12×12×12 at 0.90×, 25×25×25 at 0.96× (same day,
  same build; the record is
  [`rank3_cubes_fftw_avx2_2026-11-22`](../../tests/performance_results/rank3_cubes_fftw_avx2_2026-11-22/)).
- **Many small extents.** The more axes of 2 to 6 points a shape has, the further behind it is:
  2×2×2×2×2×2×2 at 0.48×, 3×3×3×3×3×3×3 and 3×3×3×3×3×3 at 0.73×.
